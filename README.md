## ✦ What I Build

I design and code at the intersection of **material aesthetics and generative systems** — building design languages, interactive art tools, and AI-powered experiences. My work pulls from the physical world: obsidian and ember, desert copper, lunar botanicals, crystal facets, wood grain. Each project is a design system as much as it is a piece of art.

I'm also building civic AI tools rooted in Philadelphia neighborhoods — connecting generative technology to community land use, regenerative infrastructure, and participatory design.

## 🌋 Elemental Mythpunk Series

Four generative single-page art experiences — each a distinct elemental world built with raw Canvas API, SVG kintsugi, Web Audio synthesis, and GSAP scroll narrative.

```
🌋 Volcanic Obsidian Ember  —  magma veins · ember particles · obsidian kintsugi
🏜️ Desert Mirage Nomad      —  dune engine · Berber patterns · copper kintsugi · bloom on click
🌕 Luminous Lunarpunk        —  mosaic tiles · botanical vines · golden kintsugi · goddess fragments
❄️ Arctic Frost Shaman       —  glacial crystals · silver kintsugi · tundra spirits
```

## 🎨 Design System Family

My design systems share a common philosophy: interfaces should feel **tactile, material, and joyful** — like objects you want to pick up and touch.

