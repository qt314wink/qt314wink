# JENNIPHER TROUP &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; @qt314wink

**Architectures of Coherence & Meaning**

I work at the level of underlying structure — where narrative, identity, and collective intelligence intersect. My practice is clarity: locating the coherence point in complexity and establishing the conditions for movement to become self-evident.

The work crosses generative art, behavioral design, fabrication, full-stack development, research synthesis, and therapeutic protocol architecture. The through line is a single question: *how do you design for a person standing at the edge of something they don’t yet understand, and make crossing feel worth it?*

## The Morphica Design System Family

A series of systems that translate physical material properties into browser-native UI language. Each system derives its entire token vocabulary, naming conventions, and motion logic from a single material source.

| System | Material | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| ChromaFlora | Bioluminescence, cosmic growth | Procedural glow, living light, bloom |
| Jewelmorphism | Gems, crystal, refracted light | Chromatic depth, physics interaction |
| Woodmorphism | Wood grain, organic structure | Warmth, grain flow, structural honesty |
| *Stonemorphism* | Basalt, granite, slate | Weight, permanence, geological time |
| *Papermorphism* | Folded paper, origami | Layering, crease logic, flat-to-form |
| *Fabricmorphism* | Woven textile | Warp/weft grid, thread density, drape |

*Italics = planned expansions*

## Design Doctrine

**Constraints are structural.** The limitation is not the obstacle — it’s the parameter that produces integrity.

**Failure is instructive.** Every system I build logs its own breaking.

**Joy is load-bearing.** Delight is the signal that a threshold has been crossed safely.

**Every system improves from being used.** The loop is the point.
